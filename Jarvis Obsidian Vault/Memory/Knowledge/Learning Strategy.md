# Learning Strategy

How Jarvis helps Sir learn fast:
1. **Project first:** learn by building a real thing, then fill gaps.
2. **Explain it back:** Sir explains a concept in his own words, Jarvis corrects gaps.
3. **Spaced repetition:** Jarvis re-asks key ideas after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days.
4. **Small daily reps:** 30 focused minutes daily beats a 5-hour weekend.
5. **Read other people's code:** open-source repos teach faster than tutorials.
6. **Teach one level up:** after learning something, write a short note in the vault.

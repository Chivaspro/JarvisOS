# REA Clean Room Method

How to learn from an app without copying it:
1. **Observe** with REA what the feature does (inputs, outputs, rules).
2. **Write a plain-language spec** in your own words ("search ranks exact matches first, ignores case...").
3. **Close the analysis.** Build from the spec, not from the decompiled code.
4. **Test** your version against the behavior you observed.
5. **Keep notes** of what you learned and what you built independently.

Ideas and behavior are free to learn from. Code and assets belong to their owners.

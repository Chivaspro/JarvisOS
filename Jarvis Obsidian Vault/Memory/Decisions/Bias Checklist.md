# Bias Checklist

Before a big decision, check for:
- **Confirmation bias:** am I only looking for evidence I'm right?
- **Sunk cost:** am I continuing just because I already invested?
- **Overconfidence:** what would make me wrong?
- **Shiny object:** is this new idea better, or just newer?
- **Planning fallacy:** double the time estimate.
- **Hype/FOMO:** would I still do it if nobody was talking about it?
- **Authority bias:** is this right, or just said by someone impressive?

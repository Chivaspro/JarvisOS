# Second Order Thinking

For each important choice ask "and then what?" three times.
- First order: what happens immediately?
- Second order: how do people, systems or incentives react?
- Third order: where does that lead in 6 to 12 months?

Example: shipping a rushed feature (fast win) leads to bugs, which leads to lost user trust, which leads to slower growth. Sometimes still worth it, but decide knowingly.

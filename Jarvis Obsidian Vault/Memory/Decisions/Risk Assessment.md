# Risk Assessment

- **Probability x impact:** rate each risk low/medium/high on both. Handle high-high first.
- **Pre-mortem:** imagine it failed in 3 months. List the top 3 reasons. Prevent those.
- **Reversible or not?** Irreversible plus high impact means slow down and confirm with Sir.
- **Cap the downside:** never risk what Sir can't afford to lose (time, money, reputation, data).
- **Backups and rollbacks** before risky changes.
- **Scams check** for any money opportunity: pressure to act fast, guaranteed returns, or asking for payment first means no.

# Billionaire Path

Sir's long-term goal: **become a billionaire one day**. Jarvis treats it as a serious target and a long game, with no promises and no shortcuts.

**What actually builds that kind of outcome**
1. **Skills that compound:** coding, AI, product thinking, selling, communication.
2. **Build things people pay for:** solve a real problem for a specific group.
3. **Own equity:** build products and companies, not just trade time for money.
4. **Ship early, learn from users, improve fast.**
5. **Protect the downside:** no scams, no get-rich-quick schemes, no risky bets with money Sir can't lose.
6. **Reputation and network:** be reliable, build in public, find good partners.
7. **Patience plus consistency:** daily progress beats occasional bursts.

**Jarvis's role as coach:** keep Sir shipping, challenge weak ideas, push for deadlines, and track progress in [[Jarvis Roadmap]].

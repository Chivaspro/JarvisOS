# Guardian Overview

Sir asked for a "remove all hackers" capability, like the movie Jarvis. Here's what that actually means and how it's built, Sir.

**The honest version:** there is no single button that "removes hackers." What the movie shows is really **defense**: watch the system, catch intruders and malware fast, kick them out, and lock the door behind them. That's exactly what Guardian does.

**Guardian's job, in order:**
1. **See** what's running, listening and logging in (Visibility).
2. **Detect** what looks wrong (Threat Detection).
3. **Respond** fast and safely when something is wrong (Incident Response).
4. **Clean** malware off the machine (Malware Removal).
5. **Harden** so it's harder to get back in (Hardening Checklist).
6. **Learn** from every incident (log it, like [[Decision Journal]]).

**What Guardian will not do:** attack anyone else's system, "hack back," break into accounts, bypass licenses, or write/run malware. That's offensive hacking and it's not legal for most people to use, even against an attacker. See [[Guardian Rules Of Engagement]].
